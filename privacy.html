Privacy Policy

Content Publisher · Last updated 1 November 2026

Content Publisher is a private publishing tool. It is used only by its owner to upload videos the owner created to social media accounts the owner controls. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

What we access

When the owner connects an account (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube), the platform gives the tool an access token and basic account details (account name and ID). The tool uses them only to upload and publish the owner's own videos and to check that a post succeeded.

What we store

Access tokens and account IDs are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable by the owner alone. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platform's own API.

What we don't do

We do not collect data about viewers or other users, do not use cookies or analytics on this site, and do not sell or share any data.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token files removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ydvsailendar/content-publisher.
